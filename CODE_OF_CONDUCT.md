# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We want this project to stay focused, respectful, and useful.

Be patient with people who are still learning.  
Be direct when something is wrong.  
Do not turn technical disagreements into personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, or personal insults of any kind are not welcome here.

## Enforcement

If someone is making the space worse, maintainers can warn, restrict, or remove them.  
Decisions will be made based on what keeps the project healthy, not on popularity.

## Scope

This applies to GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other spaces connected to the project.

## Contact

If you need to report something, open a private issue or contact the maintainer directly through GitHub.
