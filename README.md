## The Light Being

A floating entity made of living light that exists above the entire Android system.

| Behavior | Action |
|---------|--------|
| User speaks / commands | Materializes smoothly |
| User continues browsing | Instant elegant fade-out (voice keeps speaking) |
| Screen idle | Appears and performs stunning light acrobatics |

**Core Systems:**
- **Screen Awareness** — understands what you are doing and reacts subtly
- **Idle Performance System** — intentional, escalating light performances
- **Memory Manifestation** — visual memory echoes made of light
- **Split & Merge** — can divide into light threads then recombine
- **Presence Preference Learning** — learns how much visual presence you prefer

→ Full specification: [docs/LIGHT_BEING.md](docs/LIGHT_BEING.md)

## 🕊️ Manifesto — Why Glimmer Exists

Technology has never been more powerful.

And yet —

**152 million elderly people** live their final years in silence and loneliness.  
**970 million people** carry invisible mental illness with nowhere to turn.  
**1.3 billion people** with disabilities are left behind by devices that were never designed for them.

**We built Glimmer because technology has been talking *at* people instead of *with* them.**

Glimmer is not a feature. It is not an app category.  
**It is a commitment** — that the most powerful AI will serve every human being, in every language, on every device, under an iron-clad legal and ethical framework.

> *"The measure of technology is not what it can do — but who it includes."*
